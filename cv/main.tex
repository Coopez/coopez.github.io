\section{About Me:}

I am a machine learning researcher completing a PhD at the University of Oslo (expected 04.2027) on probabilistic forecasting for dynamic energy systems. I develop monotonic deep lattice networks that forecast full predictive distributions over multiple horizons, with validity guaranteed by the architecture. I want to extend shape-constrained neural networks towards physics-informed and trustworthy AI, and I enjoy teaching and supervising students alongside research.

\section{Post-Graduate Education:}

{\bf PhD}, \href{https://www.mn.uio.no/its/english/}{Dep. of Technology Systems}, University of Oslo  \hfill {\bf 03.2023 - 04.2027}   \\
Title: \textit{Artificial Intelligence-based Prognosis of Dynamic Energy Systems}

I work on probabilistic forecasting and uncertainty quantification for energy time series, from solar irradiance forecasting and nowcasting to battery state-of-health estimation. The project is a collaboration with the \href{https://ife.no/en/front-page/}{Institute for Energy Technology (IFE)} and the \href{https://www.ffi.no/en}{Norwegian Defence Research Establishment (FFI)}.
